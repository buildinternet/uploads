<section class="lead" id="what-it-does">

## What it does <a class="anchor" href="#what-it-does" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a>

The uploads.sh Slack app turns uploads.sh links into previews. With it in your workspace:

- A [file link](/docs/attach-pull-request-images) shows its image, the pull request or issue it
  belongs to, and its type, size, and upload time.
- A [live link](/docs/feeds) shows the pull request title and state, the latest screenshot, and
  how many files it holds.
- A [gallery](/docs/galleries) link shows its title, description, and cover image.

</section>

<section id="install">

Slack asks for two permissions: to see uploads.sh links in messages, and to add previews to
them. The app can't read anything else in your workspace. Post an uploads.sh link to see a
preview.

</section>

<section id="connect">

## Connect your account <a class="anchor" href="#connect" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a>

Open the app's **Home** tab in Slack and press **Connect uploads.sh**. Sign in to uploads.sh if
asked, then confirm. The link ties your Slack account in that workspace to your uploads.sh
account.

Previews work without connecting. The link is for features that act on your behalf. To undo it,
press **Disconnect** on the same Home tab.

</section>

<section id="privacy">

## What a preview shows <a class="anchor" href="#privacy" aria-label="Link to this section">#</a>

A preview shows only what the public share page shows. The app reads the same public endpoints
the share pages use, so private, held, and deleted files get no preview.

<div class="note">
  Previews are visible to everyone in the channel. Post a link to a private file only where
  you'd share the file itself.
</div>

</section>
